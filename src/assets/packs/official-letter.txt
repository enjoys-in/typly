OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Subject: Guidelines regarding the conduct of the Data Entry Skill Test for direct recruitment to Group 'C' posts — reiteration of instructions.

The undersigned is directed to refer to this Department's Office Memorandum of even number dated the 17th March, 2024 on the subject cited above, and to state that references continue to be received from various Ministries and Departments seeking clarification on the manner in which the skill test is to be conducted.

2. It is hereby reiterated that the prescribed speed for the Data Entry Skill Test shall be eight thousand key depressions per hour on a computer, to be assessed on the basis of a passage given for a duration of fifteen minutes. The candidate shall be permitted the use of the backspace key, and every key depression, including corrections, shall be counted for the purpose of computing speed.

3. All Ministries and Departments are requested to bring the above instructions to the notice of all concerned recruiting authorities under their administrative control for strict compliance.

4. This issues with the approval of the competent authority.
